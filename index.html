METHOD & LIMITATIONS

Anomaly = daily ERA5-Land maximum temperature minus the 1991–2020 mean for the same calendar day. Sample locations are dynamically generated within the selected EA polygons. They do not represent a continuous high-resolution temperature surface. This is a model-based screening product, not an official GMet bulletin.
